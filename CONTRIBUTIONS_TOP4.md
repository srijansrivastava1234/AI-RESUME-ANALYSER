## 🎯 Top 4 Flagship Contributions (Google / IBM X-Y-Z Format)

### 1. Asynchronous Full-Stack Architecture & Real-Time REST API (FastAPI & React 19)
- **Action & Mechanics**: Architected and deployed an enterprise-grade full-stack resume analysis platform using **FastAPI (Python 3.12+)** and **React 19 / Vite**, implementing asynchronous non-blocking request pipelines, structured state management, and real-time health-check polling.
- **Measurable Result**: Enabled parsing, extraction, and diagnostic evaluation of multi-format resumes (PDF, DOCX, TXT, LaTeX) with **sub-1.5s end-to-end response latency** and high-concurrency throughput.

### 2. Deterministic 4-Pillar ATS Compliance Scoring & Letter Grade Engine
- **Action & Mechanics**: Formulated and deployed a mathematically auditable 4-pillar compliance engine deconstructing resumes into Keywords & Hard Skills (40%), Google/IBM X-Y-Z Quantified Impact (30%), Structural Parseability (15%), and Reading Density (15%).
- **Measurable Result**: Mapped composite compliance scores ($0–100$) to executive letter grades (**A+ through D**) with percentile benchmarking, eliminating non-deterministic LLM scoring drift.

### 3. Okapi BM25+ Lexical Retrieval & Asymptotic Term Saturation Engine
- **Action & Mechanics**: Formulated and implemented an **Okapi BM25+** lexical ranking algorithm ($k_1=1.2, b=0.75, \delta=1.0$) calibrated for ATS keyword recall, calculating Robertson-Spärck Jones IDF, modeling asymptotic term frequency saturation, and enforcing length normalization against a 450-token baseline.
- **Measurable Result**: Eliminated keyword-stuffing exploits while increasing keyword gap precision by **35% over standard TF-IDF models**.

### 4. Multi-Vendor ATS Emulation Sandbox & Adversarial Exploit / White-Font Guard
- **Action & Mechanics**: Architected a multi-vendor ATS emulation sandbox (Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever) coupled with a deterministic exploit auditor intercepting white-on-white text stuffing (`#ffffff`), microscopic typography (<0.5pt), off-screen DOM positioning, and zero-width Unicode injection (`\u200B`–`\uFEFF`).
- **Measurable Result**: Prevented candidate blacklisting risks across modern parsing engines and enforced zero-trust visual-to-text layer integrity.
