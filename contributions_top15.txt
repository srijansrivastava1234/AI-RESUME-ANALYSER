   • Architected text layer diagnostic engine detecting Unicode Private Use Area (PUA) codepoints (\uE000-\uF8FF),

   • Formulated career timeline parsing standardizing dates to ISO-compatible months, calculating accurate

   • Built ontological skill classifier separating hard technical proficiencies from subjective soft buzzwords,
     enforcing a Hard-to-Soft Ratio (R_skill) to penalize buzzword dilution exceeding 30% and verifying bullet substantiation.

9. Candidate Contact RFC 5322, E.164 & HTTPS Profile Link Security Auditor
   • Engineered candidate contact coordinate validation screening against RFC 5322 email specifications,
     standardizing international phone numbers to ITU-T E.164, and auditing external profile links for HTTPS encryption.

10. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) Multi-Metric Candidate Scoring Engine
    • Formulated and deployed Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF_score(d) = sum [1 / (60 + r_m(d))]) consolidating
      disparate candidate evaluation dimensions (lexical recall, XYZ impact, hygiene, readability) into a
      mathematically normalized, outlier-resistant candidate ranking leaderboard.
